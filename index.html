How it works

Three steps from photo to phone call.

1

Upload their car

The shopper snaps or uploads a side photo of their own car. Spokeshot finds the wheels, and they can drag or resize them if needed.

2

Pick a wheel and size

They browse wheels from your catalog, then choose the wheel diameter and tire size. Overall diameter and the change from their current tire are shown as they go.

3

Call or request a quote

Quote and call buttons go straight to your shop, with the wheel and size they picked, so they can ask you about that exact setup.

FAQ

Questions shops ask.

Does it check fitment?

No. Spokeshot is a visual preview only. It shows how a wheel and tire size could look on the shopper's photo. It does not check bolt pattern, offset, width, brake clearance, load rating or rubbing. Your shop should confirm fitment before every sale, and the tool says this to shoppers too.

Where do the wheels come from?

From your own catalog. We can load it from a Shopify store, a spreadsheet or your product photos. Front-facing wheel photos work best; we prepare angled photos during setup.

Will it work on my website?

If you can paste HTML into a page, yes. That covers Shopify, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and most custom sites. The tool runs in its own isolated box, so it won't break your theme.

Do customers need an app or an account?

No. It runs in the browser on phones and computers. Shoppers don't sign up for anything.

Are customers' car photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is processed in the shopper's own browser and isn't sent to us or stored. It only leaves their device if they choose to download or share the result.

How accurate is the preview?

The wheel and tire are drawn to scale from the sizes chosen, and the shopper can fine-tune position and angle. Results depend on the photo; a clear, level side shot works best. Treat it as a styling preview, not a measurement.

Send the form below. We'll reply to set up a trial version with some of your wheels so you can try it before paying anything. No card is needed to start.

Can I cancel?

Yes, the monthly plan can be cancelled anytime. The setup fee covers the one-time work of loading your catalog and branding.
